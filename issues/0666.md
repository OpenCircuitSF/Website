## Description

User request (2026-11-14): add guides for using a multimeter, one important workshop skill. Meters come with a black and a red probe, but the jacks are not always clearly marked, which confuses novices. Cover a couple of meter variants.

- Guide 1 is setting up the probes: which jack each probe goes in.
- The other three guides each cover one measurement: continuity, volts and amps.

The user has no photos of the workshop's meters yet and will supply them on 2026-11-15. Until then, illustrate the meters as SVG drawings rendered to PNG for the web pages. The drawings are to be replaced by photos later, so keep the image paths and the jack-coordinate data easy to swap, the same way `PAD_GEOMETRY` maps the SuperMini photos.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Four new topic guides in `web/src/content/guides/guides.json`, appended after the existing topics, each with `slug`, `title`, `hook`, `minutes`, `tags` (`multimeter` plus one more) and plain `post` copy:
   - `multimeter-probes`
   - `multimeter-continuity`
   - `multimeter-voltage`
   - `multimeter-current`
2. Each has a body in `web/src/lib/topics/topics.ts` (`demo`, `tryIt`, three `ideas`, `takeaway`), written in the existing guides' plain voice.
3. **Two meter variants** are drawn as SVG and rendered to PNG:
   - **A, the budget 3-jack meter.** A manual-range rotary dial, with jacks `10A`, `COM` and `VΩmA`, the style of the common DT-830.
   - **B, the 4-jack auto-ranging meter.** Jacks `A`, `mA µA`, `COM` and `VΩ`.

   SVG sources go in `assets/guides/multimeter/`. Rendered PNGs, 1x and 2x, go in `web/public/img/guides/multimeter/`. Rendering uses `rsvg-convert`, which is installed. Never use a browser (AGENTS.md §8). A small script, `scripts/render-guide-art.sh`, regenerates the PNGs. Draw jack labels small and low-contrast, the way real meters print them, so the guide's teaching is what makes them clear.
4. An interactive demo per guide, built on one shared, data-driven meter component with jack hotspots over the PNG. The data, in `web/src/lib/topics/multimeter.ts`, maps each meter to its image, jack positions and dial positions.
   - **Probes:** pick meter A or B, then click a jack to plug the black probe, then the red one. Feedback explains why: black always goes to COM; red goes to the V/Ω jack for almost everything; and the amp jacks are only for current. Wrong choices get a specific, kind explanation.
   - **Continuity:** set the dial to continuity (the diode/beep symbol) and touch the probes to a good wire, a broken wire and a closed/open switch. It beeps (visually) or shows `OL`.
   - **Voltage:** set DC volts and measure a AA battery (about 1.5 V), a 9 V battery and the ESP32 board's 5V and 3.3V pins. Reversed probes show a minus sign. On meter A, a range set too low shows `1` (over range).
   - **Current:** the meter must be in series. Breaking the circuit and inserting the meter gives a reading of about 20 mA through an LED. Putting amp-jack probes straight across a battery shows "fuse blown", with the explanation, and is the guide's key lesson. It covers the 10A vs mA jack choice.
5. The logic (jack correctness, readings, messages) is in plain TS (`multimeter.ts`) with unit tests. The Svelte components stay thin. `demos.smoke.test.ts` covers mounting (automatic for every topic), plus one behaviour test per guide.
6. Server: OG images, the sitemap, SEO meta and `/guides` listing pick up the new topics automatically from the catalog. Confirm `go test ./internal/guides/... ./internal/seo/...` passes.
7. `ISSUE=0666 scripts/check.sh` is green, with Go and web.

## Fix

- **Catalog:** four topics appended to `guides.json` (`multimeter-probes`, `multimeter-continuity`, `multimeter-voltage`, `multimeter-current`), tags `multimeter` plus `tools`/`debugging`/`power`. Bodies in `topics.ts`.
- **Drawings:** `assets/guides/multimeter/meter-a.svg` (yellow DT-830B style, 340×612, 20-position manual dial, jacks `10A` / `COM` / `VΩmA` with `UNFUSED` and `MAX 200mA` fine print) and `meter-b.svg` (orange 4-jack auto-ranging, 380×700, SELECT/RANGE/HOLD/light buttons, 10-position dial, jacks `A` / `mA µA` / `COM` / `VΩHz`). DC, diode and beep symbols are drawn as shapes, not glyphs. Jack labels are small and low-contrast. The drawn knob has no pointer; the component overlays a rotating bar knob. `scripts/render-guide-art.sh` renders 1x and 2x PNGs with `rsvg-convert`. The SVGs were generated once by a throwaway Python script and are now the hand-editable source.
- **Logic (`multimeter.ts`):** `METERS` holds image, LCD box, dial (center, radii, positions with angle and display scales) and jacks (role, fuse rating, x/y in 1x pixel space). Swapping in a photo means changing `image` and the coordinates. `probeFeedback()` gives a kind, specific explanation for each jack. `measure()` models the display: manual ranges show a lone `1` over range, auto-ranging shows `OL`; reversed probes give a minus; continuity beeps below 50 Ω (A) or 30 Ω (B); a current jack across a battery blows its fuse when the fuse is rated below the ~3 A short (the mA jacks), while the unfused 10A jack or the 10 A-fused A jack gives a "short circuit" warning instead; a blown fuse leaves the series circuit open; a voltmeter in series blocks the current.
- **Components:** `MultimeterMeter.svelte` is shared: meter picker, image, LCD overlay, rotating knob, dial and jack hotspots as keyboard-accessible `role=button`, plugged probes with leads, a BEEP badge and a persistent `role=status` note. The demos are thin: `ProbesDemo`, `ContinuityDemo`, `VoltageDemo` and `CurrentDemo`. `CurrentDemo` adds a small schematic (9 V battery, 390 Ω, LED, about 18 mA), series vs across, and a "Replace the fuse" button. `TargetPicker.svelte` is new too.
- `internal/guides/guides_test.go`: topic counts 6→10, 7→11.

## Review notes

Reviewed 2026-11-14 (opus, medium): commits `632fb92e`, `92330e00`, `514e5c2a`. **Passes**, with one small fix made in review (`ded2ab23`).

- **Teaching content checked for electrical correctness.** Black in COM always; red in V/VΩmA for everything but current; current jacks modelled as a near-short to COM regardless of the dial, while meter A's shared VΩmA jack is a shunt only on its mA settings (correct for the DT-830 family). Manual over-range is a lone `1`, auto-ranging is `OL`; reversed probes give a minus; continuity thresholds (50 Ω / 30 Ω) are realistic. Series LED circuit (9 V, 390 Ω, ~2 V red LED) gives 17.95 mA, matching the copy. The 200 mA fuse on VΩmA and the 600 mA fuse on meter B's mA µA jack blow across a battery; the unfused 10A jack and the 10 A-fused A jack report a short, not a blown fuse. A voltmeter in series blocks the current and the LED goes dark. The 10–30 second 10A duty note is accurate.
- **Fixed in review:** on meter A, continuity on the 220 Ω resistor shows over range (its beep range tops out at 199.9 Ω), and the message said there was "no path through the 220 Ω resistor. It is open." That is wrong. A finite resistance over range now says it is a path but not a connection, and points to an Ω setting. The continuity idea's resistor sentence no longer promises the value is always shown. The probes idea "Jacks marked A, mA or 10A" could be read as including VΩmA (marked mA), so it now says "with no V". A test pins the meter A resistor case.
- **Components:** jack and dial hotspots are `role=button`, `tabindex=0`, Enter/Space operable, with `aria-label` and `aria-pressed` for the dial. The note is an always-rendered `role=status` live region. The theme tokens (`--accent`, `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`, `--bg-panel`, `--text*`) are used for all page chrome. Hard-coded fills are only on the drawn meter and leads, with a dark-mode override for the black lead in the schematic.
