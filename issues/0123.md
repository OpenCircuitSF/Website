## Description

Every campaign that has been sent gets a permanent, public, search-indexable page at `/archive/{slug}`, plus an index at `/archive` linked from Home and the footer. The slug is minted when the campaign is **created as a draft**, not when it is sent, so short links and promotional copy can be prepared against the final URL before the send goes out.

- [ ] On the campaign's transition to `sent`, `archive_status` → `published` and `archived_at` is stamped, in the same transaction as the status change

## Notes

PRD §6.8 is the specification; §5.1 and §8 carry the routes.

**Why the slug is assigned at draft time.** The user's requirement, and the reason this is not a detail: promotion for a campaign — a `go.opencircuitsf.com` short link, a Discord post, a Luma event description — is written before the send. If the slug were minted at send time the URL would not exist when the marketing needs it. Minting at draft time also means the campaign compose screen can show the copyable URL immediately.

**Attribution without tracking.** Archive URLs are the landing target for short links carrying `utm_source` / `utm_medium` / `utm_campaign`. `#0026` already captures those on signup, so promotion of a campaign gets per-channel attribution with nothing added to the email. The §2 non-goals stand: no open-tracking pixels, no per-recipient click tracking, no third-party analytics.

**`withheld` must be `410`, not `404`.** A published page that later goes away has been deliberately retracted; `410 Gone` tells crawlers to drop it, `404` invites re-crawling.
