## Description

`TestClaimCommentGuardDirectionalAxisCalibrationTable` counts only findings
where `f.ident == "ClaimDue"`, and `#0364`'s
`claimCommentGuardDiagnoseDirectional` **inherits that scope** — it hardcodes
`"ClaimDue"` in both its window check and its findings filter.

**Measured by `#0364`'s review**: forcing every **non**-`ClaimDue` identifier in
the window to flag unconditionally leaves the whole 24-row table **green**.

That includes the `every-distinct-identifier` row, whose stated purpose is that
a nearer `OrphanSweep` resolves while a farther `ClaimDue` does not. **That
row's `OrphanSweep` half is pinned by nothing.**

This is a **pre-existing consequence** of the table's `ClaimDue`-only count
scope, not introduced by `#0364`, and outside its criteria.

## The decision this issue must make

Two defensible outcomes, and the review that found this said so explicitly:

1. **Add a second count/reason pair scoped to the other identifier** on that one
   row — narrow, targeted, closes the measured hole.
2. **Record the limit and stop** — the table's scope is `ClaimDue`, say so
   plainly and note what that leaves unpinned.

Given the `#0330` / `#0347` / `#0356` precedent, option 2 may well be right.
**But it should be a decision, not an accident** — which is the whole reason
this is filed rather than left implicit.

Whichever is chosen, `#0364`'s own caution applies: adding an external harness
to pin the reason assertion would be the seventh in-file check that issue's
caution warns about, and `#0364`'s reviewer already considered and **rejected**
that. Do not reach for it here either.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The choice above is made explicitly, with its reason recorded in the guard's

|---|---|---|
