going to be, not what it is. `#0419`'s implementation pass read it directly and
reports six divergences:

| no `twitter:title` / `twitter:description` | both exist |

`handlers.SPAHandler`'s embedded-file lookup, with no `internal/seo` involvement.

## Why it matters more than an ordinary stale doc

**Other documents defer to it.** `docs/seo.md` cited §7.4 as authoritative until
`#0419` rewrote it — and that rewrite had to record §7.4's unreliability in
prose rather than fix it, because `PRD.md` was owned by a concurrent pass at the
time. So the tree currently contains a document explaining that its own cited
authority is out of date.

The "per path" caching line is the sharpest instance: a reader following §7.4
would reimplement the memory-unbounded behaviour `#0073` was filed to remove.

**Two imprecisions, recorded rather than bounced.** Neither is the
`#0073`-class harm this issue was filed over, and both are one-line fixes
for whoever next touches these files:

- §7.4's closing sentence describes the card route as serving "a published
  workshop with no `cover_image`". Eligibility is `workshopIsCardable` —
  `Status == WorkshopPublished && Published` — and does **not** consider
  `cover_image`: the route returns a card for a published workshop that has
  a cover image too; the cover image only wins for `og:image`.
  `docs/seo.md`'s "**`cover_image` still wins**" bullet has this right. Read
  as a statement of the card's *purpose* the sentence is defensible, which
  is why this is a note and not a bounce. Suggested wording: "… at `GET
  /workshops/{slug}/og.png` for a published workshop, used as its `og:image`
  when it has no `cover_image`."
- `docs/seo.md`'s new intro says "§7.4 stays a short summary by design
  (`PRD.md`'s own convention, §11)". A reader follows that to `PRD.md` §11,
  which is Security and Compliance; the intended target is `CLAUDE.md` §11,
  and that section documents a section *index*, not a summary-length
  convention. Suggested wording: drop the parenthetical, or make it
  "(`CLAUDE.md` §11 is the index of those sections)".

Also noted: `docs/seo.md`'s history bullet says §7.4 "didn't mention
`#0273`'s per-workshop card generation, its route, or its `og:type` decision
at all". The rewritten §7.4 covers the first two; the `og:type` rationale
stays in `docs/seo.md` only. That is a defensible split for a summary
section, but the bullet reads as if all three were closed.
