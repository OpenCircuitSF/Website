## Description

From the Fable outreach content review,
`docs/reviews/outreach-content-review.md`, proposed issue A. Brennan,
2026-10-17, on the generated Announce drafts: "This will be clearly seen as
poorly automated content which will be quickly ignored or even blocked. We
cannot do this." Read the review's §2 to §5 for the reasoning and the
example posts.

## Acceptance criteria (from the review, §6 A)

- `outreach.Facts` carries `Summary` (trimmed `workshops.summary`) and, when
  present, the outreach flyer `tagline`; `outreachFacts` fills them and
  `Fingerprint` includes `Summary`.
- `Draft` produces a prose lead that contains the weekday, date, time and
  venue in a sentence (no `·` separators, no `New workshop:` label), followed
  by the summary (truncated at a sentence boundary to fit the channel), the
  channel's CTA, then the hashtag line. Three waves have distinct leads that
  read as written by a person.
- Bluesky and Mastodon drafts are never byte-identical for the same facts;
  a Go test asserts it.
- A Go test pins the Programming-LEDs-shaped fixture's output for each
  channel × wave (golden strings), within each channel's limit after the
  short link is substituted.
- The `Regenerate` path produces the new text; existing edited drafts are
  untouched.

## Relation

- Follows `#0544`, `#0545`, `#0568` and `#0569`. Source:
  `docs/reviews/outreach-content-review.md` §6 A.

## Resolution

Rewrote `outreach.Draft` (`internal/outreach/templates.go`). `Facts` gained `Summary`, `Tagline` and `Hashtags`; `outreachFacts` fills `Summary`; `Fingerprint` includes the whitespace-normalised summary and tagline (hashtags are deliberately not an input, so a Settings save does not stale every draft). Each draft is a prose lead carrying weekday, date, time and venue, then the summary cut at a sentence boundary (or a word, with an ellipsis) to fit the channel after the short link is counted as Mastodon counts it, then the CTA, then the hashtag line. Bluesky and Mastodon use different leads and CTAs per wave, so they are never byte-identical (tested, including undated and venueless facts). Bluesky and Instagram drafts carry no link, so `SetPrepared` no longer appends a URL to those channels. Instagram and the video channels got their templates here too (#0576 criteria). `TestDraftGolden` pins the Programming-LEDs-shaped fixture. Decision: the flyer `tagline` is only a fallback when there is no summary, and the handlers do not yet pass it (the tagline lives on the flyer row and threading a flyer lookup through eleven call sites was not worth it for Monday); `Facts.Tagline` and `Fingerprint` honour it for when that is wired. Existing posts are untouched; only new drafts and Regenerate use the new text.
