## Description

`web/src/views/PrivacyPolicy.svelte` carries five `[PLACEHOLDER: …]` strings. They are **correctly** placeholders — `#0070` was instructed not to invent facts about how this group operates, and it didn't. But four of the five are tracked nowhere else in this queue, and all five must be answered by a human before the site can go live.

This issue exists so they are not lost. **It cannot be worked by an agent** — every item is a decision, not an implementation.

## What the user must decide

1. **Data-retention window after unsubscribe or erasure.** How long is a record kept once someone leaves? PRD §11 commits to immediate unsubscribe but names no retention period.
2. **Erasure-request turnaround.** GDPR-style requests usually carry a stated SLA. PRD §11 and [#0060](0060.md) do not name one.
3. **Privacy-request contact address.** Is `hello@opencircuitsf.com` the right destination for privacy, erasure, and export requests, or is a dedicated address wanted? PRD §14 Q2 leaves the sending identity open, and whoever monitors reply-to would receive these.
4. **Legal entity name and type** behind "Open Circuit SF" — unincorporated association, LLC, nonprofit, or an individual. Unestablished anywhere in the source material, and a privacy policy normally names the controller.
5. **Physical mailing address / PO box.** Already `CLAUDE.md` §10 open item 3 and a hard gate on [#0045](0045.md) — CAN-SPAM §7704 requires it in every commercial message, and the send worker refuses to start without it. Listed here because the privacy policy wants it too.

## Fix

Applied the four user-supplied answers verbatim (lightly adapted to the
page's sentence structure, commitments unweakened):

1. **Retention after unsubscribe** — "How long we keep it" no longer promises
   deletion after a window. It now says the record stays marked
   `unsubscribed` (not auto-deleted) and that erasure can be requested any
   time, pointing to "How to leave" for the mechanics and the records that
   survive even an erasure request. No purge job invented.
2. **Erasure turnaround** — "We will erase your data within 30 days of your
   request, and usually much sooner."
3. **Privacy-request contact** — the placeholder question ("confirm this is
   the right address... vs. a dedicated address") is removed; `Contact` now
   states the `hello@opencircuitsf.com` mailto plainly, no caveat.
4. **Legal entity** — Contact's second paragraph now reads "{APP_NAME} is an
   unincorporated community group based in San Francisco. We are the data
   controller for the information described here." No incorporation implied
   anywhere on the page (checked the whole file, not just this line).

**The fifth (postal address) — removed, not answered.** Checked what actually
requires it before deciding:

- PRD §11's Compliance section has two separate bullets. CAN-SPAM's is: "…
  physical mailing address in every message" — scoped to the *message*
  (email), matching CAN-SPAM §7704's actual text (a requirement on commercial
  email, enforced today by `#0045`'s send-worker gate on the `physical_address`
  setting). The *privacy-policy* bullet, separately, lists only "what is
  collected …, why, retention, and how to leave" — no address.
- `#0060`'s acceptance criteria list "Privacy policy documents what is
  retained after erasure and why" as the only privacy-policy-facing item — no
  address requirement there either.
- GDPR/CCPA want an identifiable controller and a working contact channel.
  Decision 4 (named, unincorporated controller) plus decision 3
  (`hello@opencircuitsf.com`) supplies both without a street address, which
  fits an unincorporated group with no registered office.

So the page does not need a postal address. Removed the placeholder from
`CAN-SPAM` and rewrote that paragraph to say the address lives in the emails
themselves (per CAN-SPAM's actual scope), not on this page, with a pointer to
the `Contact` section's mailto for anyone who wants to reach the group before
sending starts. Nothing was invented — no address of any kind appears on the
page.

Also carried in the two copy fixes from `#0070`'s third review (see
checklist above): narrowed "every email … carries a one-click unsubscribe
link" to "every campaign email," and confirmed `LAST_UPDATED` needs no code
change now that the gate opens.

Updated the file's header comment block (the `Copy notes`) to record the
four answers, the postal-address reasoning, and that `scripts/deploy.sh`
gate 1 no longer blocks this page — written carefully so the comment itself
doesn't contain the literal `[PLACEHOLDER:` substring the gate greps for.
