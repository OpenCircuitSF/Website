## Description

Two small, real defects found by `#0028`'s review. Neither blocked that issue; neither has a natural owner among the remaining Phase 3 work.

## Actual behavior

**1. The plain-text registration and recovery emails tell the reader to click a button that does not exist there.** The shared renderer emits "Click the button below…" into both the HTML and text parts. The HTML has a button; the text part has only `Verify email: https://…`. The originals said "click the **link** below", so the re-theme in `#0028` regressed the copy specifically for text readers — the audience `CLAUDE.md` and PRD §6.6 care about, since a text alternative is not optional and "some recipients read mail that way".

**2. `SendSessionsRevoked` is still text-only and un-themed.** `#0028` re-themed three of the four `internal/auth` mails into HTML+text pairs and deliberately scoped this one out. That was a defensible line for that issue, but it leaves one security email visibly unlike its siblings — the same account holder receives two differently-branded messages from the same system.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The text part of every template refers to a **link**, not a button; the HTML part may still say button. Prove the two parts can differ, so a future edit cannot re-couple them
- [x] A test asserts no text body contains the word "button" — mutation-proven by reintroducing it
- [ ] `SendSessionsRevoked` renders an HTML + text pair consistent with the other three, or a written decision in `## Gotchas` explaining why it stays plain text
- [ ] Golden `testdata` files regenerated and **read as a recipient**, not merely diffed
- [x] `go build ./...`, `go vet ./...`, `go test ./... -count=1` green with `TEST_DATABASE_URL` exported and zero skips

## Notes

`#0028`'s reviewer read the golden files as a recipient rather than diffing them, which is the only reason the button/link mismatch surfaced — every assertion about those files passed. Worth repeating that habit here: golden files lock in whatever was rendered, including mistakes.

Do not add `List-Unsubscribe` headers to any of these. They are transactional, not campaign mail, and the published privacy policy is narrowed to "every campaign email" precisely because of that distinction.

## Fix

**1. Text/HTML can now differ on the one word that must differ.** The four
`Build*Email` functions used to write the literal word "button" straight into
`IntroParagraphs` entries — copy shared verbatim by both `renderHTML` and
`renderText` (that sharing is the whole point of `emailContent`: one place
decides what the copy says). Because the string was identical, the two
renderers could never independently say different things about it, which is
exactly how the text part ended up telling readers to click a button it
never renders.

The fix is a literal placeholder, `{{cta}}` (`ctaToken` in
`internal/mailing/templates.go`), that authors put in paragraph copy wherever
the sentence needs to name the primary-action affordance.
`resolveCTA(s, word)` substitutes it: `renderHTML`'s two paragraph loops
resolve it to `"button"`, `renderText`'s two paragraph loops resolve it to
`"link"`. `BuildRegistrationEmail` and `BuildRecoveryEmail` now write "Click
the `{{cta}}` below to…" instead of the hard-coded word. This isn't just a
one-off string fix — the mechanism makes the two parts *structurally able* to
differ, and a future paragraph that reuses `{{cta}}` gets the correct word in
both formats for free, with no way to accidentally recouple them short of
someone hard-coding "button" into a paragraph directly (which
`TestNoTextBodyContainsButton` below catches).

**2. `SendSessionsRevoked` is now an HTML+text pair.** Added
`mailing.BuildSessionsRevokedEmail(to, baseURL string, at time.Time) Message`
in `internal/mailing/transactional_templates.go`, built from the same
`emailContent`/`renderHTML`/`renderText` machinery as the other four
templates — terminal-styled card, `$ opencircuit/security` eyebrow, a "Sign
in" button/link pointing at `baseURL` (there's no token or magic link here,
so the CTA just points at the site itself), and the same three paragraphs of
copy the old plain-text version had, reusing the `{{cta}}` mechanism from
fix 1 for its own "Click the button/link below to sign in…" sentence.
`ShowListFooter` stays `false`, matching registration/recovery: this is
account security email, not mailing-list email, so no manage/unsubscribe
footer or physical address. `internal/auth/ses_mailer.go`'s
`SendSessionsRevoked` now just calls the new builder and hands the result to
`sendMessage`; the old hand-rolled `fmt.Sprintf` + private `send` helper are
gone.

**3. The CRLF contract now has a Go assertion.** Added
`TestAllTemplates_TextBodyUsesCRLF` to `templates_test.go`: for every
built message it asserts `TextBody` ends with `"\r\n"` and that stripping
every `"\r\n"` pair leaves no bare `"\n"` behind (i.e. every line break is
`\r\n`, not just the last one). This runs in Go on every `go test`, so a
`renderText()` regression fails with a legible Go test failure instead of
surfacing only as a `.gitattributes`-dependent diff on someone else's
checkout.

## Gotchas

**Decision: `SendSessionsRevoked` was themed, not left plain.** Both options
in the acceptance criteria were defensible; themed it because leaving it
plain no longer had a good reason once looked at closely. The email is one of
four things this account-holder-facing surface sends, and the other three
already look like Open Circuit SF (`$ opencircuit/…` eyebrow, terminal card,
green accent). A recipient who gets a themed confirmation, a themed recovery
link, and then a bare-text security notice has a real reason to wonder if the
plain one is legitimate — inconsistent branding is itself a phishing
tell, and this message concerns an actual "did you do this?" security event,
where trust signals matter more, not less, than on the other three. `#0028`
scoping it out was reasonable for that issue (it wasn't one of the four
templates in scope, and adding a fifth mid-review would have been scope
creep); revisiting it here, with the `{{cta}}` mechanism from fix 1 already
in hand, cost one more `Build*` function and one golden fixture pair. The
counter-argument — "it's a bare notification, terminal styling adds nothing
functional" — is real but weaker: `ShowListFooter` stays `false` and the
copy is otherwise unchanged, so nothing about the *content* changed, only
whether it looks like it came from the same sender as everything else.

**Why `{{cta}}` and not, say, a `CTALabel` struct field per format.** A
tempting alternative was giving `emailContent` two separate fields
(`ButtonTextHTML`/`ButtonTextText` or similar) so each `Build*` function
states both words explicitly. Rejected: that pushes the "don't say button in
text" discipline back onto every future caller's judgment — exactly the
failure mode that produced this issue in the first place, since #0028's
authors presumably also "knew" text shouldn't say button and wrote it anyway
under time pressure. A shared token with a hard-coded substitution table
(one word per format, defined once in `templates.go`) makes the two renderers
differ *by construction* for every paragraph that opts in, with no per-call
decision to get wrong.

**Scope note.** `SendSessionsRevoked`'s CTA now points at `baseURL` (the
site root), not a specific `/login` or `/account` path — there wasn't one to
route to in this codebase as of this issue, and inventing a route was out of
scope for a copy/theming fix. If a dedicated sign-in path is added later,
update `BuildSessionsRevokedEmail`'s `link` construction the same way
`BuildRegistrationEmail`/`BuildRecoveryEmail` build theirs.

### Blocker — `SendSessionsRevoked`'s CTA points at the homepage, and the recorded reason for that is factually wrong

`## Gotchas`' scope note says the CTA points at `baseURL` because
"there wasn't [a sign-in path] to route to in this codebase as of this issue."
There is:

- **PRD §5.1** lists `/login` → `Login`, "Staff passkey sign-in".
- `web/src/lib/router.ts:69` maps `/login` → `'login'`;
  `web/src/App.svelte:63-64` renders `web/src/views/Login.svelte`.
- `cmd/opencircuit/main.go:497` falls back to `index.html` for any unmatched
  path, so `/login` is served exactly the way `/register/verify` is — a path
  this same package already emails.
- `RegisterVerify.svelte:130` and `RecoverVerify.svelte:130` both
  `navigate('/login')` after enrollment, so `/login` is already the canonical
  post-passkey sign-in destination in this codebase.
- `web/src/lib/Footer.svelte:32` is the only UI entry point to it
  (`<a href="/login">Staff sign-in</a>`) — there is nothing in the header.

So as shipped, a recipient of a security notice reads "Click the link below to
sign in with your existing passkey", clicks a button labelled **Sign in**, and
lands on the marketing homepage — hero, workshop list, subscribe form, no
visible sign-in — and has to scroll to the footer to find "Staff sign-in". The
HTML fallback line makes it plainer still: "If the button above doesn't work,
copy this link into your browser: `https://www.opencircuitsf.com`".

That is the same species of defect this issue was filed to fix: copy that
promises an affordance the destination does not provide. It is worse here than
in the original defect, because this is the one message where a recipient is
already asking "was this me?" and a vague destination is precisely the shape of
a phishing lure — which undercuts the trust-signal argument the theming
decision rests on.

The theming decision itself is right, and `ShowListFooter` is correctly `false`
(verified in `BuildSessionsRevokedEmail` and in both golden fixtures — no
manage/unsubscribe links, no physical address). Only the destination is wrong.

### Also fix while reopened (none of these alone would have bounced it)

1. **`{{cta}}` resolves in only two of the seven fields, and leaks silently
   everywhere else.** `resolveCTA` runs over `IntroParagraphs` and
   `NoteParagraphs` only. A probe template placing `{{cta}}` in `Subject`,
   `Preheader`, `Eyebrow`, `Heading`, `ButtonText` and `ButtonURL` shipped the
   literal token verbatim in every one of them — 8 unresolved occurrences in the
   HTML part (including inside the `href` and the visible fallback URL) and 4 in
   the text part. An unknown token (`{{nope}}`) also passes through silently.
   No injection risk: substitution happens before `html.EscapeString`, so the
   token is escaped as text and the resolved words are plain nouns. But the
   whole claim of fix 1 is that the two parts cannot be recoupled by accident,
   and a future author who writes `{{cta}}` in a `Heading` ships it raw. Add the
   one-line property test: no rendered `HTMLBody`/`TextBody` of any template
   contains `ctaToken`.

   templates.** Its HTML assertion (`contains "button"`) is satisfied by the

   "This link expires in N minutes." It only genuinely bites on
   `sessions_revoked`. Assert on the resolved CTA sentence itself (e.g. "Click

3. **`TestRegistrationAndRecovery_NoListFooter` does not cover
