# 0639 — Outreach tests seed workshops on a fixed 2026-12-05 date and may start failing after it

## Description

Found while planning `#0638`. Outreach handler tests seed their workshop on 2026-12-05 while the handlers read the real clock. `#0638` pins the clock for the hand-links tests (a `beforeEvent()` helper) and moves the SPA fixture to 2099, but other tests in the outreach suite may also quietly assume today is before 2026-12-05, and will fail on or after that date for no code reason.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Find every outreach Go and SPA test whose outcome depends on the seeded date being in the future (run the suite with the clock or seed date moved past 2026-12-05 to find them).
- [ ] Make the seed date relative to now, or pin the clock, so the suite gives the same result on any date.

- Follows `#0638`.
