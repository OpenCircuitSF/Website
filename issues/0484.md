## Description

`#0402`'s criterion 6 asks that all the auto-login states be exercised through
the real `mountAndServe` route table. `TestNewDevAdminAutoLogin_RealRouteTable`
covers three — no credential, flag off, and the one-row session cache. The two
that matter most for `#0409`'s defect, **a stale cookie and a garbage cookie**,
are pinned a layer down against a fake resolver rather than through the real
table.

Both were exercised live, twice, by `#0402`'s review and again by `#0409`'s
verification pass. **A live run is not a regression pin.** Nothing in the suite
fails if the real route table stops healing them.

## The reason given for stopping does not hold

`#0409`'s implementation pass declined to extend the test and gave three costs.
Its reviewer checked them, and two do not survive:

1. **"Needs a real `sessions` row insert and delete."** It does not. A token
   that never existed and a token whose row was deleted are **the same
   server-side state** — `ResolveSession` fails identically. The insert/delete
   round trip buys nothing.
2. **"It is an ~8×-cost package."** That figure is the cost of *running*
   `cmd/opencircuit`, which the standard verification scope pays anyway
   (`CLAUDE.md` §5a). Adding two cases to a test already in that package costs
   approximately nothing extra.
3. The third — needing a cookie-jar-free client — is already satisfied:
   `TestNewDevAdminAutoLogin_RealRouteTable` **already holds** `pool`, the
   server URL, and a jar-less client.

So the remaining work is two `http.NewRequest` plus `client.Do` calls with
explicit `Cookie` headers, and their assertions. The reviewer put the real
remainder at **~20 lines**.

**Declining was still right on scope** — it was outside `#0409`'s bounce
remedy, and a pass that grows past its remedy is how `#0474` reached three
rounds. What is wrong is the recorded justification, which would stop the next
reader from attempting a cheap thing by telling them it is expensive.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Extend `TestNewDevAdminAutoLogin_RealRouteTable`** to cover the stale-cookie
   and garbage-cookie states through the real route table, so both are pinned
   rather than only ever measured live.
2. **Verify the two-states-are-one-state claim** before relying on it. If a
   deleted row and a never-existing token are genuinely indistinguishable to the
   resolver, say so in the test's own comment, since that is what makes the
   cheap form correct.
3. **Prove the new cases can fail.** Restore the presence-only short-circuit in a
   throwaway worktree and confirm both new assertions fail. A test never seen to
   fail is not a test (`CLAUDE.md` §8). Never `git stash` (§8a).
4. **Do not grow the pass.** No new middleware behaviour, no new states beyond
   the two named. `#0258`'s spiral and `#0474`'s three rounds are both on record.
5. Check whether the **json-path sibling** deserves the same treatment, and say
   yes or no with a reason. `DevAutoLogin`'s healing tests live in
   `internal/middleware`; whether they warrant a real-route-table equivalent is
   a judgement, not an obvious yes.
