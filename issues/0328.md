## Description

`#0320` added a content digest so an in-place migration edit is no longer
invisible. Its review verified the mechanism thoroughly — fail-closed on every
malformed `shasum` output, safe under every stray-file shape tested, digest
reproduced across four locales and from a different absolute path. Three
residuals, none of them a defect today.

### 1. `template_digest` costs two extra `psql` round trips

`#0320`'s reviewer measured `create` at **0.182s** before and **0.243s** after —
criterion 6 met, but the margin is thin, and **only 0.017s of the +0.061s is the
digest itself**. About 0.04s is two extra `psql` invocations, because
`template_digest()` re-runs a `db_exists` check that `template_version()` already
did, then opens a second connection.

Reading version and digest in one query recovers most of it.

### 2. The `pg_shdescription` join is under-qualified

It lacks `and ds.classoid = 'pg_database'::regclass`. That catalog is keyed
`(objoid, classoid)` and also holds role and tablespace comments, so an OID
collision across catalogs could match the wrong row. Fails safe today — a wrong
digest routes to provisioning — but it is one clause.

### 3. `find | sort | xargs cat` is not NUL-delimited

A migration filename containing a space or a quote breaks `xargs`. The reviewer
tested it: the digest comes back empty, `valid_digest` catches it, and `create`
falls back to provisioning with `xargs: unterminated quote` on stderr. **Fails
safe**, and no such filename exists or is likely. `find -print0 | sort -z |
xargs -0 cat` closes it.

## An operational note that is not an acceptance criterion

The shared `opencircuit_test_template` **predates `b0aa902`**, so it carries no
digest comment and `template_digest` returns `none`. **Every `create` on this
tree currently mismatches and takes the ~0.26s direct-provision path.** It
self-heals the moment someone runs `scripts/testdb.sh template` explicitly, at a
quiet moment. That is expected transitional behaviour, not a defect — but it is
also exactly what made an observer conclude the template was being rebuilt
implicitly (it was not; `#0320`'s reviewer disproved that three ways).

## Acceptance criteria

1. `template_version` and `template_digest` share one `psql` invocation and one
   existence check. Re-measure `create` and report the number; the target is
   recovering most of the ~0.04s.
2. The `pg_shdescription` join is qualified by `classoid`.
3. The digest pipeline is NUL-delimited end to end.
4. **None of these may weaken the fail-closed behaviour** `#0320`'s review
   verified: empty, non-hex, short, and uppercase `shasum` output must each still
   refuse the clone and provision from disk, and a missing `shasum` must still
   route to fallback. Re-run all five cases after changing the pipeline.
5. Every stray-file case still fails **toward** provisioning, never toward a
   false match. The reviewer's table is in `#0320`'s review notes — re-derive it.
6. `ISSUE=NNNN scripts/check.sh guards` passes, run at least twice.
