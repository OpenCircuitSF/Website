## Description

`cmd/opencircuit/shutdown_wiring_test.go`'s
`TestMountAndServe_SIGTERMReleasesInFlightClaim` failed twice on 2026-10-18
and passed on rerun with nothing changed: once during `#0588`'s verification
(while svelte-check ran concurrently) and once during `#0593` part A's (load
about 6). Neither run was investigated.

AGENTS.md §5 (`#0470`): a deadline-bound test failing at normal load is
evidence of a defect, not of a busy machine. Look first for a silent decline
path — a worker that `continue`s without logging, an early return on an empty
result, or a background goroutine outliving the test that started it.
Outreach added background workers this phase (the outreach promoter /
publisher queue kind `outreach_post`, the engagement tracker with
`OUTREACH_TRACKER_INTERVAL`), which may now share the claim path or
`outbound_queue` rows with this test. Earlier history: `#0081`, `#0084`,
`#0126`, `#0254`.
