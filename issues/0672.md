### Facts checked

- **ESP32-C3 Series Datasheet v2.5** (read with pdftotext from Espressif's PDF):
  - Radios: 2.4 GHz 802.11 b/g/n and Bluetooth 5 (LE).
  - Table 5-7: TX peaks of 335 mA (802.11b @21 dBm), 285 mA (g) and 276 mA (n HT20); RX 84 mA.
  - Table 5-8: Modem-sleep at 160 MHz with the CPU running draws 23–28 mA.
  - Table 5-9: Light-sleep 130 µA, Deep-sleep 5 µA.
  - GPIO0–5 are in the VDD3P3_RTC domain.
- **ESP-IDF v5.4 sleep-modes docs** (esp32c3):
  - `esp_sleep_enable_timer_wakeup(uint64_t time_in_us)`.
  - `esp_deep_sleep_enable_gpio_wakeup(uint64_t mask, mode)`, with `ESP_GPIO_WAKEUP_GPIO_LOW/HIGH`, on RTC IO pins only.
  - `esp_deep_sleep_start()`.
  - `RTC_DATA_ATTR` puts variables in RTC memory.

### Assumptions

- **TX burst at 8.5 dBm = 200 mA** (`TX_LOW_MA`). The datasheet gives no reduced-power figure, so this value is illustrative.
- **Average while awake with WiFi = 100 mA; without WiFi, 25–30 mA.** These blend the datasheet's RX, TX and Modem-sleep figures. They were not measured.
- **SuperMini overhead while asleep:** regulator about 50 µA, power LED about 1 mA. These are typical values that vary between board batches, and the copy tells readers to measure their own.
- **The brownout model is illustrative.** It is not a circuit simulation of any specific board. Its values:
  - The board resets below about 3.6 V on the 5V pin. This is approximate: it depends on the LDO's dropout and on the brownout level the firmware sets.
  - Cable plus port resistance is 0.5 Ω for a good cable and 3.5 Ω for a thin cable on a weak port.
  - The board already has 22 µF on it.
- **A wrong password gives `WL_CONNECT_FAILED`.** The copy notes that some core versions report `WL_DISCONNECTED` instead.
- **USB serial disappears during deep sleep.** This is well-known behaviour of the USB Serial/JTAG peripheral, but the sleep-modes page does not say it.
- **`esp_deep_sleep_enable_gpio_wakeup`** exists and is not deprecated in IDF v5.4. IDF "stable" (5.5+) documents `esp_sleep_enable_gpio_wakeup_on_hp_periph_powerdown` instead. The arduino-esp32 version in PlatformIO's platform uses an IDF where the older name still exists.
- **The WiFi guide has four ideas, not three.** The SuperMini antenna fix got its own card, and the grid in `TopicGuide.svelte` handles any number of cards.
- **The LED service and characteristic UUIDs** come from Arduino's well-known BLE LED example.
