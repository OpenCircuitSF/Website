## Description

Carved out of `#0585` item 3 by its review. ShortLinks' campaign stats
(`internal/clicks/stats.go`, three daily queries) bucket clicks by UTC day,
and `GET /api/campaigns/{slug}/stats` takes only `from`/`to`. Pacific days
cannot be rebuilt from UTC day totals, so Results stays labelled UTC. The one
remaining two-zone spot: a post marker sits on its UTC day while the post
table shows Pacific (6:30 PM PT Oct 31 → marker on "Nov 1").

**Blocked on ShortLinks** (a separate repo and tracker, `AGENTS.md` §1): add
`tz` (IANA name, default UTC, unknown zones rejected) to the stats endpoint,
group the three daily queries in that zone, and read `from`/`to` as dates in
that zone.

## Acceptance criteria (once ShortLinks ships `tz`)

- [ ] The Results proxy sends `tz=America/Los_Angeles`.
- [ ] `admin_outreach_results.go` computes "today" and the partial-last-day
      flag in Pacific (`outreachUTCDate` callers).
- [ ] `outreachResults.ts` places post markers by Pacific date.
- [ ] "(UTC)", "Days are UTC." and "Date (UTC)" in `OutreachResults.svelte`,
      `OutreachClicksChart.svelte` and `outreachResults.ts` read Pacific.
- [ ] `internal/devmocks` honours `tz`. Tests for an evening-PT post landing on
      its Pacific day. Verified with `ISSUE=0594 scripts/check.sh` scoped runs.
