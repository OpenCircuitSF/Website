## Description

From the Fable outreach content review,
`docs/reviews/outreach-content-review.md`, proposed issue E. Brennan,
2026-10-17, on the generated Announce drafts: "This will be clearly seen as
poorly automated content which will be quickly ignored or even blocked. We
cannot do this." Read the review's §2 to §5 for the reasoning and the
example posts.

## Acceptance criteria (from the review, §6 E)

- Draft all drafts **Announce** for every channel by default; Reminder and
  Last call are checkboxes (unchecked) in the Draft all control and in the
  Schedule all preview.
- `SuggestedAt` returns a time at a configurable posting hour (default
  10:00 `America/Los_Angeles`) on the day N days before the workshop,
  constructed with `time.Date` in the Pacific location, with a test across
  the 2026-11-01 DST boundary.
- The scheduled status line shows weekday **and date** ("Mon Oct 12, 10:00 AM").
- `summaryLine` no longer reports "15 posts drafted" as the headline; it
  reports posts per wave that exist.

## Relation

- Follows `#0544`, `#0545`, `#0568` and `#0569`. Source:
  `docs/reviews/outreach-content-review.md` §6 E.

## Resolution

Draft all drafts Announce for every channel; Reminder and Last call are unchecked checkboxes beside it (request body `{"waves":[...]}`, unknown wave is a 400), and unchecked in the Schedule all preview, which only schedules ticked optional waves. Slots of an undrafted optional wave are no longer listed as "not drafted" skips. `SuggestedAt(w, startsAt, hour)` is `hour:00` Pacific on the Pacific calendar day N days before, built with `time.Date`; hour comes from the new `outreach_post_hour` setting (default 10, validated 0-23, live). `TestSuggestedAtAcrossDST` covers 2026-11-01. The scheduled status line reads "Posts automatically Mon, Oct 12, 10:00 AM". `summaryLine` reports "Drafted: Announce 5, Reminder 2 - 0 scheduled - 0 posted" (posts per wave that exist). Schedule all with nothing ticked disables its button.
