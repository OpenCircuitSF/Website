## Description

The persistent shell around every public page: logo, primary navigation, and a footer carrying the legal and social links.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Header with the monochrome logo mark, wordmark, and nav links to Workshops, About, Subscribe
- [ ] Active route is visually indicated and marked `aria-current="page"`
- [ ] Mobile layout: nav collapses or wraps cleanly at ≤ 480 px with ≥ 40 px tap targets
- [ ] Footer with Discord link, contact address, privacy policy link, and copyright
- [ ] Staff sign-in is reachable but unobtrusive — a footer link, not primary nav
- [ ] Signed-in staff see an Admin link in the header
- [ ] Keyboard navigable end to end with a visible focus ring on every interactive element
- [ ] A skip-to-content link is the first focusable element

## Notes

PRD §5.1, §7.5. The privacy policy link is required — the site collects email, interests, signup IP, and UTM source, and #0060's erasure flow needs a documented home. If the policy page does not exist yet, file a follow-on rather than linking to nothing.
