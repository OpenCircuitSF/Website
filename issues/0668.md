## Description

`#0666` drew the two meters in SVG, rendered them to PNG, and asked for them to be replaced with real photos. The user will supply photos of the workshop's meters on 2026-11-15.

The swap:

1. Put the photos in `web/public/img/guides/multimeter/`, at 1x and 2x, as WebP or PNG.
2. In `METERS` in `web/src/lib/topics/multimeter.ts`, update each meter's `image` and its LCD, dial and jack coordinates. They are measured in the 1x image's pixel space.
3. Drop or retarget the test in `multimeter.test.ts` that checks the jack coordinates against the SVGs.
4. If the workshop's meters differ from the two variants that were drawn, adjust the jack and dial data and any copy that names a jack.
