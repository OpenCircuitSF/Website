`/opt/opencircuit` is **711 commits behind** `main`. That is not itself a defect

`#0435`'s pass found this independently for its own scripts and documented a
`scp` pre-step. `#0447`'s and `#0465`'s instructions were written without it, and
`#0447` is already `resolved` — so the correction has to reach a closed issue's
recorded instruction, not just an open one.

## Why this is one issue and not three

The three instructions share a single missing precondition. Fixing them
separately invites three different spellings of it, and `#0431`'s lineage is a
standing demonstration of what happens when the same fact is maintained in
several places by hand.

There is also a second-order point worth settling once: **`#0435` chose `scp` and
a SHA-256 comparison; a `git pull` would bring everything at once.** Those are
different trades — `scp` touches exactly the files named and needs no network
access from the box to GitHub; `git pull` is one command but moves 711 commits,
including a binary rebuild's worth of source. Pick one per instruction with a
stated reason rather than by habit.

3. Decide `scp`-versus-`git pull` **per instruction**, with the reason recorded.
   `#0435` already chose `scp` for three files and verified by hash; do not
   silently change it, but say whether it is still right alongside the others.
4. `#0447` is `resolved`. Amend its recorded instruction **additively** —
   `#0421`'s precedent for touching settled content — and do not reopen it
   unless the amendment changes what was decided rather than how it is run.
5. **Nothing runs on the box.** Read-only confirmation only; these are
   instructions for the user (§5b, §9).
6. Check whether any *other* prepared instruction in the tracker has the same
   shape. One missed precondition is weak evidence the rest are complete.
