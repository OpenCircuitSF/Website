## Description

The user's request, 2026-11-06: "I feel like the home page of Outreach could list all scheduled posts for each upcoming event. That would help me see what will be done and what is missing. It could be presented on a timeline with the posts and events showing on this timeline."

Context: on 2026-11-05 the November newsletter fell back to a plain link for `hack-night-2` because Outreach had never been started for it. Nothing on the Outreach home made that gap visible.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Outreach home (`#/outreach`) shows a timeline of upcoming published events, with each event and its posts (Announce, Reminder and Final, by channel, including hand-posted video) placed at their scheduled or posted times.
- [ ] Each post shows its status: drafted, scheduled or posted. Gaps are obvious: an event with no Outreach started, a section with nothing scheduled, or a channel missing a post.
- [ ] Each event and post links to its Outreach page or section.
- [ ] Desktop only (user decision 2026-11-05): no phone layout work.
