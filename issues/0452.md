## Description

`mailing.opencircuitsf.com` carries a live TXT record:

    v=spf1 include:amazonses.com -all

`docs/email-setup.md` documents it correctly. **`docs/deployment.md`'s DNS
table does not mention it at all.**

Measured read-only by the `#0427`/`#0448` reviewer via
`aws route53 list-resource-record-sets` while re-deriving the whole zone.

## Why it was reported rather than folded into `#0448`

`#0448`'s criterion 3 asked to **sweep the existing rows** — to find rows that
say something untrue. This is an **omission**, not a false statement, so the
reviewer judged it outside that scope and reported it instead of widening a
passing sweep. That is this phase's settled precedent (`#0425` → `#0437` →
`#0440`): report, do not widen.

## Why it is worth closing

SPF is one of the three records that decide whether list mail is delivered or
junked, alongside DKIM and DMARC — and the table already carries the other two.
An operator rebuilding this zone from `docs/deployment.md` recreates DKIM and
DMARC and **silently omits SPF**, at which point SES mail from the `mailing.`
subdomain fails SPF alignment. The failure appears as deliverability decay
rather than as an error, which is the hard kind to attribute.

The two documents disagreeing is itself the defect: `docs/email-setup.md` is
right, so a reader who consults only the deployment runbook gets an incomplete
zone with no cue that anything is missing.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `docs/deployment.md`'s DNS table carries the `mailing.` TXT row with its real
   value, **re-derived from the zone** rather than copied from this issue
   (`CLAUDE.md` §7's standing instruction) or from `docs/email-setup.md`.
2. Note the `-all` hard-fail qualifier explicitly if the table has room for a
   comment — it is a deliverability decision, not a default, and a future editor
   softening it to `~all` should have to do so knowingly.
3. Sweep for **any other record present in the zone but absent from the table**,
   the mirror image of `#0448`'s sweep. Say what you found, including "nothing
   else".
4. **Change nothing in DNS.** This is a documentation fix. **Never touch the
   apex MX** — it carries real Google Workspace mail (`CLAUDE.md` §9).
5. Do not restate what `docs/email-setup.md` already covers well; the goal is
   that the runbook's table is complete, not that it duplicates the other doc.

### What I re-derived, and what matched

Re-derived read-only with `aws route53 list-resource-record-sets
--hosted-zone-id Z0825067RV8QY5UIKS96`. Local `dig` was not attempted —
outbound port 53 is blocked in this sandbox, as this issue already records.

Both SPF values match the table exactly, so the `-all`/`~all` contrast the row
draws is real and is described correctly:

- `mailing.opencircuitsf.com` TXT — `v=spf1 include:amazonses.com -all`
- `bounce.mailing.opencircuitsf.com` TXT — `v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all`

The row's framing is also technically right: `bounce.mailing.` is the custom
MAIL FROM name, so calling it the envelope-domain row is accurate, and
`mailing.` is the `From:` header domain. The note records the asymmetry as a
deliberate choice not to be softened casually and proposes no DNS change,
which is what criterion 2 asked for. Nothing in the diff touches DNS — it is
two files, `docs/deployment.md` and this issue — and the apex MX is unread and
unmodified. No DKIM selector token, the apex `google-site-verification` value,
or the Workspace DKIM public key appears in either file; re-derived by
byte-scanning both with `python3` for all five strings, zero hits.

### Defect 1 — the zone has 15 record sets, not 16

The paragraph asserts the zone "returns 16 record sets". It returns **15**,
confirmed three independent ways: enumerating `ResourceRecordSets` from the
listing (15 entries, no `IsTruncated` key, so the page is complete);
`--query 'length(ResourceRecordSets)'` (15); and `aws route53
list-hosted-zones`, which reports `ResourceRecordSetCount: 15` for the sole
public zone named `opencircuitsf.com.` — there is no second or private zone
for this domain that could account for the difference. The 18 individual
`ResourceRecords` across those 15 sets (the apex `NS` set alone holds four
values) is the only nearby number, and it is not 16 either.

This is the exact class of statement `#0427` and `#0448` existed to remove
from this table — an assertion about production that measurement contradicts
— reintroduced by the pass meant to complete the sweep. It is also
load-bearing rather than decorative: the count is the denominator the
paragraph's completeness argument rests on, so a reader who re-runs the
command and gets 15 cannot tell whether a record was deleted or the sweep
miscounted.

The commit message of `0e4d62d` carries the same error as "the zone's other
15 record sets" (it should read 14). Do not rewrite that commit; just do not
repeat the figure in the new one.

### Defect 2 — six records are left unaccounted for

After "one was a genuine omission", the paragraph says "The rest are
deliberately left out of this table, not missed" and then lists bullets
covering eight records: the apex `NS` and `SOA`, the apex `MX`, the apex
`TXT`, `google._domainkey.opencircuitsf.com`'s `TXT`, and the three DKIM
`CNAME`s. That leaves six record sets which are neither the new row nor in
any bullet, and for which the sentence is false — they are not left out of
the table at all, they are already documented rows:

- `opencircuitsf.com` `A`
- `*.opencircuitsf.com` `CNAME`
- `www.opencircuitsf.com` `CNAME`
- `_dmarc.mailing.opencircuitsf.com` `TXT`
- `bounce.mailing.opencircuitsf.com` `MX`
- `bounce.mailing.opencircuitsf.com` `TXT`

That those six are present is the sweep's actual finding — the reason nothing
else is missing — and omitting it is what makes the arithmetic fail to close.
1 + 6 + 8 = 15, and the paragraph as written offers only 1 + 8.
