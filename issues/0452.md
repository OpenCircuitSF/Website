## Description

`mailing.opencircuitsf.com` carries a live TXT record:

    v=spf1 include:amazonses.com -all

`docs/email-setup.md` documents it correctly. **`docs/deployment.md`'s DNS
table does not mention it at all.**

Measured read-only by the `#0427`/`#0448` reviewer via
`aws route53 list-resource-record-sets` while re-deriving the whole zone.

## Why it was reported rather than folded into `#0448`

`#0448`'s criterion 3 asked to **sweep the existing rows** — to find rows that
say something untrue. This is an **omission**, not a false statement, so the
reviewer judged it outside that scope and reported it instead of widening a
passing sweep. That is this phase's settled precedent (`#0425` → `#0437` →
`#0440`): report, do not widen.

## Why it is worth closing

SPF is one of the three records that decide whether list mail is delivered or
junked, alongside DKIM and DMARC — and the table already carries the other two.
An operator rebuilding this zone from `docs/deployment.md` recreates DKIM and
DMARC and **silently omits SPF**, at which point SES mail from the `mailing.`
subdomain fails SPF alignment. The failure appears as deliverability decay
rather than as an error, which is the hard kind to attribute.

The two documents disagreeing is itself the defect: `docs/email-setup.md` is
right, so a reader who consults only the deployment runbook gets an incomplete
zone with no cue that anything is missing.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `docs/deployment.md`'s DNS table carries the `mailing.` TXT row with its real
   value, **re-derived from the zone** rather than copied from this issue
   (`CLAUDE.md` §7's standing instruction) or from `docs/email-setup.md`.
2. Note the `-all` hard-fail qualifier explicitly if the table has room for a
   comment — it is a deliverability decision, not a default, and a future editor
   softening it to `~all` should have to do so knowingly.
3. Sweep for **any other record present in the zone but absent from the table**,
   the mirror image of `#0448`'s sweep. Say what you found, including "nothing
   else".
4. **Change nothing in DNS.** This is a documentation fix. **Never touch the
   apex MX** — it carries real Google Workspace mail (`CLAUDE.md` §9).
5. Do not restate what `docs/email-setup.md` already covers well; the goal is
   that the runbook's table is complete, not that it duplicates the other doc.
