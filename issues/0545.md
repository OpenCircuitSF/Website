## Description

Phase 2 of the Outreach design: **Post now** and **Schedule** for the two
automatic channels, sent through the durable outbound queue with retries, and
the resulting post's id/URL recorded on the `outreach_posts` row.

Design doc section "Posting to Bluesky and Mastodon, and manual channels":
https://claude.ai/artifact/DzCWVhfcqJbc9uSfWLVfhG

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Bluesky: app-password session (`com.atproto.server.createSession`), cached and refreshed (createSession is capped at 300/day); `com.atproto.repo.createRecord` with `app.bsky.feed.post`; link and hashtag **facets** with correct UTF-8 byte offsets; an `app.bsky.embed.external` card (URL, title, summary, cover thumbnail uploaded via `uploadBlob`, ≤ 1,000,000 bytes or skipped). 300-grapheme limit enforced before sending.
- [ ] Mastodon: `POST /api/v1/statuses` with bearer token and an `Idempotency-Key`; link preview left to the instance.
- [ ] Plain `net/http` clients (no `indigo`); unit tests against `httptest` fakes, including facet offsets for non-ASCII text.
- [ ] Config, all optional: `BLUESKY_HANDLE`, `BLUESKY_APP_PASSWORD`, `BLUESKY_PDS_URL` (default `https://bsky.social`), `MASTODON_BASE_URL`, `MASTODON_TOKEN`. A channel without credentials shows as "not configured" and falls back to manual. `.env.example` and `docs/configuration.md` updated.
- [ ] Scheduled/now posts are enqueued on the existing outbound queue with a new kind, reusing its backoff/abandon policy; success sets `posted`, `posted_at`, `remote_id`, `remote_url`; exhaustion sets `failed` with `last_error` and a **Retry** action.
- [ ] Unsent posts are held (not sent) when the workshop is canceled or unpublished.
- [ ] UI: Post now / Schedule (default times per wave) and Retry in the composer and panel.
- [ ] Verified with `ISSUE=0545 scripts/check.sh`; no real network calls in tests.
