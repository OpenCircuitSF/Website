## Why neither was fixed by `#0508`

`#0508`'s scope boundary applies — the same reasoning that protected 140
dated mentions across the tracker. Item 1 in particular describes *how a fact
was measured at filing time*, and `CLAUDE.md` §1, the authority it cites, is
already correct.

They are filed rather than dropped because item 1 is a **Go doc comment in a
live guard test**, not an issue transcript, so the usual "dated record"
protection is a weaker fit: nobody reads it as history the way they read a
resolved issue file.
