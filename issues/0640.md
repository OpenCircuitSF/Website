## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A section (Announce, Reminder, Final) is scheduled with **one** date and time that applies to both Bluesky and Mastodon. Per-channel times are not offered by default.
- [ ] Choosing a section shows only that section; the other two are not on the page until chosen.
- [ ] A summary view lists every post (each section × channel, including the hand-posted video channels) with its status: drafted, scheduled (with time) or posted (with time).
- [ ] It is clear at a glance what the site will do and when.
- [ ] Existing scheduled and posted rows in production are untouched by the change.
