## Description

The newsletter archive (`#0123`) has no feed. Measured on production, 2026-09-12: `/feed.xml`, `/rss.xml`, `/archive/feed.xml`, and `/archive.rss` all return 404.

A feed lets people follow the newsletter in a feed reader without subscribing by email, and gives aggregators and search engines a single, always-current list of new issues. It suits a newsletter archive especially well.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `GET /archive/feed.xml` serves a valid **Atom** feed with `Content-Type: application/atom+xml`. Atom rather than RSS 2.0 because its dates and entry IDs are strictly specified; record the choice.
2. It lists **published campaigns only** — the same predicate as the archive list, the sitemap, and the archive pages (`#0318`, `#0519`), newest first, capped at a stated number of entries.
3. Each entry has a stable, permanent `id` that never changes when a campaign is edited, a `link` to its archive page, `title` from the subject, `published` and `updated` from `archived_at`, a `summary` (`#0528`'s description text), and the full newsletter as `content` from the same web renderer the archive page uses (`RenderMarkdownPageHTML`), correctly escaped for XML.
4. Every public page's `<head>` carries `<link rel="alternate" type="application/atom+xml" title="Open Circuit SF newsletter" href="…/archive/feed.xml">`, so browsers and readers discover the feed.
5. **Withholding a campaign removes it from the feed immediately**, through the same `Invalidate()` path that updates the sitemap and archive pages (`#0319`, `#0325`).
6. The feed contains nothing private: no recipient data, no unsubscribe tokens, no internal IDs.
7. Admin-entered text cannot break the XML. Test with hostile subjects and bodies.
8. **Post-deploy, verified by the user:** the W3C Feed Validation Service accepts the live feed.
9. `ISSUE=NNNN scripts/check.sh go ./internal/seo/... ./internal/handlers/... ./cmd/opencircuit/...` passes with no skips.
