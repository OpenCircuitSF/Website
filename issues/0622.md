## Description

On a workshop's Outreach screen, preview cards are titled only with the channel and account, so the same title appears three times. The wave is named once at the top of a section about 1,800 px tall, and nothing stays pinned. Four copies of the cover photo take 31% of a 6,414 px page. The Reminder header can show a past suggested date beside its real scheduled time, and the Announcement header has no status at all.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every preview card names its channel and wave, such as `Bluesky · Reminder`.
- [ ] A wave strip with the ordinal, wave name and actual date and time stays pinned while its section is on screen. A wave with no time says it is not scheduled.
- [ ] Each wave has distinct non-colour cues: ordinal, name and timing. Only existing brand tokens are used.
- [ ] Previews show a small cover thumbnail by default, with a control that opens the full-size preview.
- [ ] A suggested date no longer appears once a wave is scheduled or posted, and a past suggested date is never presented as upcoming.
- [ ] The Announcement header shows status in the same way as the other two waves.
- [ ] The admin screen labels the third wave **Final**. The stored wave name `last-call` and generated post copy do not change.

## Notes

The review's sketch says `the day before` for Reminder, but `internal/outreach/channels.go` suggests Reminder 7 days before and Last call 1 day before. Show dates from real data, never fixed phrases. "Final" follows the user's own words; they never call it "Last call".
