# 0602 — Bluesky thumbnail: apply EXIF orientation before re-encoding

## Description

From `#0601`'s review. When a cover is over 1 MB, the publisher
(`internal/outreach/thumbnail.go`) re-encodes it as JPEG. That drops EXIF,
including the orientation tag, so a phone photo that relies on the tag goes
out sideways on Bluesky. Under-limit files are sent byte-identical and are
unaffected.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Before re-encoding, read the JPEG EXIF orientation (1–8) and rotate or
      flip the decoded image to match. Use stdlib, or a small parser; don't
      add a heavy dependency.
- [ ] Tests: an oversized JPEG tagged with orientation 6 comes out with its
      width and height swapped. An untagged one is unchanged.
- [ ] Verified with `ISSUE=0602 scripts/check.sh go ./internal/outreach/... ./internal/db/...`. Zero skips.
