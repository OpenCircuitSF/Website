# 0268 — The citation guard does not scan the sections where claims are actually made

## Description

`#0265`'s guard resolves test-name citations in issue files. Its
`extractNamedSections` admits exactly two headings: `## Verification` and
`## Implementation notes`.

**That is not where most claims live.**

- A **fix pass** writes its evidence under `### Verification` *nested inside*
  `## Fix pass` — three of those exist today, none matched.
- A **reviewer** writes under `## Review notes`.

Both defects that bounced `#0265` a second time were in those unscanned
sections, as was the dangling citation one of them concerned.

**Measured by that review: 14 dangling citations in unscanned sections of
`open`/`in-progress` files today, against 0 in the scanned ones.**

So the guard currently enforces a rule over the smallest and cleanest part of
the corpus, while the sections where implementers and reviewers make their
strongest claims — *"I ran this and it printed that"* — go unchecked. That is
close to the opposite of the intent.

**This is not "scan everything".** `## Description` legitimately quotes names
that do not exist — `#0126`'s fabricated citations are quoted verbatim in
several issues as the subject matter. Scanning it would produce exactly the
day-one noise `#0265` scoped its way out of. The fix is to admit the sections
where evidence is *asserted*, not those where it is *discussed*.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `## Review notes`, and `### Verification` nested under `## Fix pass`, are scanned — as are any other headings where evidence is asserted; enumerate them from the corpus rather than guessing
- [ ] `## Description` and `## Notes` remain unscanned, and the reason is written down so the exclusion is not mistaken for an oversight
- [ ] **Measure before choosing.** Report the dangling-citation count per candidate section across `open`/`in-progress` files, and let that drive which are admitted — the way `#0265`'s own scope decision was driven
- [ ] The 14 known dangling citations are fixed, or each is shown to be legitimately quoted subject matter
- [ ] Mutation-proved: plant a fabricated citation in a `## Review notes` section of an open file, confirm it is named; restore, prove byte-identity with `shasum -a 256`
- [ ] `go test ./internal/db/...` passes with zero skips against an isolated database

## Notes

Found by `#0265`'s second phase-3 review under `CLAUDE.md` §9.

**Expect this to fail loudly on first run**, and treat that as the point rather
than a problem: the 14 are real dangling citations in live issue files. Fixing
them is part of the work, not a prerequisite to it.

Note the guard will then scan the very sections this issue's own fix pass and
review will write. That is a feature — `#0265`'s own notes tripped both this
guard and `#0196`'s sibling while being written, and the discipline that
resolved it (do not spell a name you cannot resolve; describe it instead) is the
one everyone should be under.
