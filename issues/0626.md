## Description

Instagram, TikTok and YouTube cards live in a collapsed **Post by hand** row at the bottom of the page, with video channels collapsed a second time. **Copy caption** copies the post body, and whether that holds the real ShortLink is not established for each channel.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts cards appear inside their wave, and the bottom **Post by hand** row is gone.
- [ ] Each card has one copy button whose clipboard text is the full paste-ready post, including that platform and wave's ShortLink and never a raw `{link}` token.
- [ ] Copying each of the three channels in one wave yields three different URLs.
- [ ] The card shows the text and its ShortLink before copying, and marking a post as posted still works.
- [ ] If the ShortLink is not available, the card says so instead of copying text with a missing link.
- [ ] The Instagram card still offers its flyer tools.

## Notes

Depends on `#0623` and `#0625`. The Instagram copy action should make clear that it is for Reels.
