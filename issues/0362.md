## Description

Two low-priority residues in `CLAUDE.md` §8's satisfier entry, both found by
`#0357`'s review and **deliberately left below that issue's bar** rather than
fixed silently (its criterion 3 says report, do not fix).

**1. A stale imperative.** The value-receiver paragraph ends:

> Any guard modeled on this one must scan both receiver forms.

Written when `#0337`'s guard was an AST scan. Since `#0349` the guard is
`go/types.Implements` over the package's full named-type set, and the
receiver-form special cases, the embedding tripwire and the `embeddingReviewed`
allowlist were **all deleted**.

The imperative is **not false** — a `go/types` guard covers both forms
automatically, satisfying it vacuously — and the clause immediately after names
`go/types` as the sound oracle, so a reader of the whole sentence is not misled.
But **quoted alone it implies the current guard does something it no longer
does**, which is the same staleness family as `#0356`.

**2. A category slip** in the same paragraph: "that exact zero-parameter,
zero-result **name**". A signature has parameters and results; a name does not.

Neither inverts, which is why `#0357` left them.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The imperative describes what a guard must *achieve* (cover both receiver
   forms) rather than what it must *do* (scan them), so it stays true of a
   `go/types` oracle and of an AST scan alike.
2. The category slip is corrected — the property belongs to the signature, not
   the name.
3. **Re-read the whole entry adversarially afterwards** and report any further
   sentence that is correct in place but implies something false when quoted.
   Report, do not fix silently. That convention is why this issue exists.
4. Documentation only. `## 8a.` through EOF must be **byte-identical** — sha256
   `29f13915…301ecb76` at the time of filing, and `internal/db/prd_section_index_test.go`
   guards §11's table inside it. The entry stays at the end of §8 before
   `## 8a.`, and no other §8 entry moves.
5. Any factual claim touched is re-verified from `git show` or a measurement,
   not copied from this issue. **This entry was published with its central claim
   inverted once** (`#0346`), and on 2026-08-31 five separate issues received a
   wrong identifier because prose was transcribed as though measured.

## The guard's current shape, for reference

Verified by `#0357`'s review: since `#0349` it is `go/types.Implements` over the
full named-type set via `golang.org/x/tools/go/packages` with
`GOOS=linux GOARCH=arm64` pinned to production (§7). The receiver-form cases,
the embedding tripwire and its allowlist are gone — `types.Implements` resolves
promotion and generic receivers on its own, and `#0353` closed the alias gap
structurally with `types.Unalias`. `#0354` and `#0355` then pinned the alias
branch's disjuncts and two of its three `continue` exclusions.

Measured by that review, for the record — a naive AST declaration scan accepting
both receiver forms finds `[Base]`, while `go/types.Implements` finds
`[*Base *EmbedIface *EmbedStruct *FeedCache EmbedIface EmbedStruct Iface]`.
