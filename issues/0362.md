## Description

Two low-priority residues in `CLAUDE.md` §8's satisfier entry, both found by
`#0357`'s review and **deliberately left below that issue's bar** rather than
fixed silently (its criterion 3 says report, do not fix).

**1. A stale imperative.** The value-receiver paragraph ends:

> Any guard modeled on this one must scan both receiver forms.

Written when `#0337`'s guard was an AST scan. Since `#0349` the guard is
`go/types.Implements` over the package's full named-type set, and the
receiver-form special cases, the embedding tripwire and the `embeddingReviewed`
allowlist were **all deleted**.

The imperative is **not false** — a `go/types` guard covers both forms
automatically, satisfying it vacuously — and the clause immediately after names
`go/types` as the sound oracle, so a reader of the whole sentence is not misled.
But **quoted alone it implies the current guard does something it no longer
does**, which is the same staleness family as `#0356`.

**2. A category slip** in the same paragraph: "that exact zero-parameter,
zero-result **name**". A signature has parameters and results; a name does not.

Neither inverts, which is why `#0357` left them.

## Root cause

Both residues trace to the same event: `#0349` replaced the AST declaration
scan in `internal/seo/invalidator_satisfier_guard_test.go` with
`go/types.Implements` over the package's full named-type set, which checks
`types.Implements(target, ...) || types.Implements(types.NewPointer(target),
...)` (method-set membership on both `T` and `*T`) rather than walking
declaration syntax for pointer- and value-receiver forms. The `#0346`/`#0350`
prose describing the guard's behavior and the property of
`(*seo.Sitemap).Invalidate` predated that rewrite and was never revisited when
it landed, so the imperative kept describing an action (scanning) the guard no
longer performs, and the earlier "name" wording (a category slip independent
of `#0349`) went unnoticed until `#0357`'s adversarial re-read.

## Fix

Two one-word edits to the existing value-receiver paragraph in `CLAUDE.md`
§8's `#0325`/`#0337` satisfier entry, no restructuring:

1. `"already carried that exact zero-parameter, zero-result name"` →
   `"...zero-result signature"` — the property (zero parameters, zero
   results) belongs to a signature, not a name.
2. `"Any guard modeled on this one must scan both receiver forms"` →
   `"...must cover both receiver forms"` — describes what a guard must
   *achieve*, true of a `go/types.Implements` oracle (verified against
   `internal/seo/invalidator_satisfier_guard_test.go:267`, which checks
   `types.Implements` on both `target` and `types.NewPointer(target)`) and of
   an AST scan alike, rather than the scanning technique specific to the
   guard `#0349` replaced.

Net `+2/-2`, two hunks, both inside the value-receiver paragraph. Did not
touch the first (satisfier-table) paragraph, `internal/seo/invalidator_satisfier_guard_test.go`,
or any other file.
