## Description

Found in `#0626`'s review. This is the most direct remaining gap against the user's complaint in `#0622`: "far easier to distinguish between posts for announce, reminder and final".

1. `draftParts` gives Instagram, TikTok and YouTube one lead regardless of section, so a Final post has no last-chance wording and only its link differs.
2. Hand-posted rows show no date. The strip gives the automatic time and `3 left to post by hand`, but a Reminder video row does not say when to post it.
3. The Video group starts about 1,430 px down at 1280 px wide and 1,900 px at 390 px, below the Bluesky and Mastodon previews, and rows are about 350 and 590 px tall.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each section's draft text for the three hand-posted channels clearly differs in wording, without changing existing drafts unless the user asks for that.
- [ ] Each hand-posted row states when to post, from the section's suggested or scheduled time, worded so that it does not read as an automatic schedule.
- [ ] Video can be reached without scrolling past the previews, by a jump link or a higher position, and rows are more compact.
