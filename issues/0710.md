## Description

User request, 2026-11-17: "Each guide also has a heading which is the LED strip. Can we change it to use the new images instead?" Clarified: "The image at the top of each card which is a series of colored boxes."

On `/guides` (`web/src/views/GuidesIndex.svelte`), every guide card starts with a `MiniStrip` (a row of coloured boxes, `web/src/lib/topics/MiniStrip.svelte`). Since `#0709` (v0.15.0) every guide has its own 1200×630 image, embedded from `internal/seo/cardassets/guides/<slug>.png` and served at `/guides/{slug}/og.png`. The cards should show that image in place of the strip. The workshop guide card (`/workshops/programming-leds`, the `workshops` section) should do the same: its art is `programming-leds.png`, served at `/guides/programming-leds/og.png`.

Things the plan must settle:
- **Weight.** 43 images at 70–265 KB each is several MB for one page. Consider a smaller thumbnail (e.g. a width-limited variant served by the Go side) and `loading="lazy"` plus explicit `width`/`height` so the grid doesn't shift. PRD §7.6 is the performance budget.
- **Cache-busting.** `og:image` carries `?v=<hash>` (`#0709`); the SPA doesn't know the hash. Decide whether the card URL needs it (the route already sends an ETag and `max-age=3600`).
- **Fallback.** A guide added later without art: keep `MiniStrip` for it, or show the generated title card the route already falls back to.
- **Alt text.** Decorative (`alt=""`) since the card already carries the title, unless the plan argues otherwise.
- The static no-JS fallback for `/guides` (`internal/seo` fallback-copy guards, AGENTS.md §5 table) may need the same change or an explicit decision not to.
- The strip inside the guide page's "Try it with real hardware" box is **out of scope**; the user means the card image only.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each guide card on `/guides` shows that guide's image at the top, in place of the `MiniStrip`; the workshop guide card shows `programming-leds`'s image.
- [ ] The page stays reasonable in weight (the plan states the measured total for the 43 card images) and images load lazily without layout shift.
- [ ] A guide with no art still renders a sensible card (the chosen fallback is tested).
- [ ] Web tests cover the card markup; `scripts/check.sh web` passes, plus any Go packages touched.
