## Plan

New file `v1_post_performance.go`.

| Tool | Method, path | Scope | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| `get_post_performance` | GET `/api/v1/posts/performance` | engagement:read | input: `since` and `until` (default the last 7 days, at most 90), `kind` (event, guide or all) |

- **Each row:** kind, title (event or guide), wave or slug, channel, remote url, posted_at (Pacific), the latest likes/reposts/replies/quotes with `captured_at`, and clicks.
- **Clicks:** from the stats caches already used by results (`AdminOutreachHandler.fetchStats` and `AdminGuidePostsHandler.fetchStats`), with `clicks_error` when ShortLinks is down (`MayBeUnavailable`).
- **Totals:** per channel and per kind, plus a one-sentence summary like `get_engagement`'s.
- **Difference from `get_engagement`:** `get_engagement` gives new activity in a window; this gives cumulative results for the posts made in a window.

**Tests:** a seeded event post and guide post with snapshots and the window filter; ShortLinks down still returns 200 with `clicks_error`.
