## Description

`#0460` gated `issueStatus`'s fence closer on indentation but deliberately kept
its **trailing-text** leniency — a closer carrying trailing text still closes —
justified in the reworded doc comment as "closing it would trade a loud failure
for a silent one".

**That is the same one-sided direction argument `#0460` was filed to retire, one
level out.** `#0455` kept the closer lenient because closing early "can only
un-hide a row"; `#0460` exists because the closer's leniency feeds a *later
opener*, producing a dangerous divergence neither branch produces alone. The
trailing-text half is defended by the identical reasoning and, measurably, has
the identical flaw.

## The measurement, which is also the acceptance evidence

`#0460`'s reviewer generated **22,620 documents** — every sequence of length 1–4
over a 12-token fence alphabet, each followed by a metadata table — and scored
them against a purpose-written CommonMark fenced-code-block reference:

| | agrees | safe divergence | dangerous divergence |
|---|---|---|---|
| pre-`#0460` | 12,199 | 9,141 | 1,280 |
| post-`#0460` | 15,727 | 6,147 | **746** |

A large net improvement — **but not monotone.** 660 dangerous documents were
fixed and **126 became newly dangerous** that were correct before. All 126 are
length 4 and all contain a fence line indented four or more columns. **96 of the
126 require the trailing-text closer**; the other 30 require the tilde or
fence-length blindness `#0455`'s criterion 3 protects.

Minimal witness: opener at column 0, an indented triple-backtick, a
triple-backtick with an info string, then a bare triple-backtick. CommonMark
closes once and reads the row; the current walk hides it.

## Scope and the stopping rule

**Corpus exposure is nil** — every genuine issue file carries its status row at
line 5 with nothing fence-shaped above it — and has been through this whole
chain. This is fidelity work on a parser, not a live defect.

`#0460`'s review reported rather than bounced, on the precedent by which
`#0455`'s review reported `#0460`: no implementation obeying the stated criteria
could have avoided those 126.

**This should be the last level of this chain unless the measurement says
otherwise.** `CLAUDE.md` §8 records `#0258` spending six passes hardening a file
against shapes the previous pass had been shown, each addition buying the next
bounce. The distinguishing fact here is that the numbers are converging rather
than circling — 1,280 → 746 dangerous, with a named residual and its exact
composition. Criterion 4 exists to keep it that way.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The closing branch requires the fence line to carry no trailing text after
   its backtick run, reaching parity with `docTableRowLines`' closer on the
   half `#0092` hardened and `#0460` declined.
2. **Re-derive the 22,620-document measurement** before and after. Do not copy
   the figures above — every number inherited without measurement in this
   repo's recent history has been wrong at least once.
3. Confirm the four safe-direction controls and every earlier probe still hold:
   `#0454`'s fenced-example-row case, `#0455`'s three opener cases, and
   `#0460`'s three-line sequence.
4. **The change must reduce the dangerous count and must not create a new
   newly-dangerous set larger than the 30 attributable to tilde and
   fence-length blindness.** If it does, stop and report rather than adding a
   fourth level — that is the stopping rule, and reporting it is the correct
   outcome, not a failure.
5. Re-derive the corpus counts and confirm **zero classification changes**
   across all issue files. `#0460`'s review measured 462 files, 461 with a
   status, 30 `open`/`in-progress`.
6. **No new mechanism, no new helper, no new file** (§8, `#0258`). Do not
   refactor `docTableRowLines` — `#0455`'s criterion 2 and `#0176`'s `wontfix`
   both still stand.
