## Resolution

New `web/src/lib/pacificTime.ts` (one module): `formatPacificDateTime`, `formatPacificWeekdayTime` (Intl, `timeZone: 'America/Los_Angeles'`, "PT" suffix), and the pure `toPacificInput` / `fromPacificInput` converters. `formatDateTime` (`lib/admin.ts`) and `weekdayTime` (`lib/outreachCards.ts`) now delegate to it, so every admin screen that formats an instant (workshop list, editor, outreach header, Results, audit, campaigns) renders Pacific. The workshop editor Starts/Ends, the clone dialog's New start/New end, the composer's Schedule input and the announcement's Schedule input are labelled "(Pacific time)" and convert through `pacificTime.ts`; `getTimezoneOffset()` is gone from the codebase (`toLocalInputValue` / `fromLocalInputValue` in `lib/outreach.ts` lost their offset parameter; `toDatetimeLocalValue` / `fromDatetimeLocalValue` in `lib/workshopAdmin.ts` are thin wrappers). DST policy: an ambiguous wall time (2026-11-01 01:30) resolves to its first occurrence (PDT, 08:30Z); a spring-forward gap time rolls forward (2026-03-08 02:30 becomes 03:30 PDT). Converted instants are now emitted without milliseconds (`...:00Z`), which the API's RFC 3339 parser accepts.

**Item 3 (Results by Pacific day) is NOT done, and cannot be done in this repo.** The per-day click counts come from ShortLinks' `/api/campaigns/{slug}/stats`, which buckets with `date_trunc('day', clicked_at AT TIME ZONE 'UTC')` (ShortLinks `internal/clicks/stats.go`, three queries) and takes no timezone parameter. Daily UTC buckets cannot be re-split into Pacific days here, and shifting the `from`/`to` window by Pacific date would drop today's UTC clicks on a Pacific evening. The Results note therefore stays "(UTC)", which remains the honest label. Needs a ShortLinks change (a `tz` query parameter, or Pacific bucketing) and then a follow-up here: orchestrator to file.
