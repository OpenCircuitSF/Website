## Description

Three comments in `web/src/lib/liveRegionGuard.structuralGuard.test.ts` (around
lines 411, 555, and 1546) say *"the two real `{#key activeId}` blocks
(`Workshops.svelte`, `Campaigns.svelte`)"*.

**There are three**: `Workshops.svelte:162`, `Campaigns.svelte:186`, and
`Campaigns.svelte:190` — `Campaigns.svelte` has two. The slip is files counted
as blocks.

**The load-bearing half is correct**, and `#0306`/`#0307`'s review verified it
independently by AST scan: none of the three wraps a live-region element, and
all three wrap exactly one child component. So `#0307`'s conclusion that the
`KeyBlock` fix is latent stands, and nothing is broken.

What makes it worth fixing rather than ignoring: **the sentence advertises itself
as measured** — "checked via the same AST walk, not assumed" — while carrying a
count that was not. A comment that claims to be a measurement is relied on as
one, which is `#0263`'s class.
