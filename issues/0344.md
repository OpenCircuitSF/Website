## Description

`Create → Confirm → Unsubscribe → RestartSignup → Unsubscribe again while
pending` yields components `(confirmed 0, imported 0, unsubscribed 1)` — a net
**−1** for one person who is, at the end, exactly where they started.

**Pre-existing**, and measured identical on both sides of `#0336`'s fix, so it
is neither caused nor worsened by it. `#0324` fixed the *four*-action version by
making `RestartSignup` clear `unsubscribed_at`; the fifth action re-stamps it
with no live arrival left to offset it.

`#0336` incidentally fixes the import-sourced counterparts — its cells O3 and P3
— because `unsubscribed_30d` excludes `source = 'import' AND consent_basis IS
NULL`. A `signup_form` row has nothing to exclude it, so the same shape survives
on the public path.

Low severity: it requires five sequential actions by one person inside a 30-day
window. It is worth fixing because it is the *third* cell of this family found
by a different method than the last — `#0311` found the first by review,
`#0324`'s review found rows O and P by building a truth table, and `#0336`'s
review found this one only by asserting the three components separately instead
of the net.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The five-action website-signup sequence nets 0, component-wise, not merely in
   the net figure.
2. The fix does not reintroduce `#0324`'s four-action asymmetry or `#0336`'s
   rows O and P — re-run their truth tables and show every cell unchanged except
   the intended one.
3. Assertions are **component-wise** (`confirmed`, `imported`, `unsubscribed`
   separately). A net-only assertion is not acceptable: `#0336`'s review found
   two cells that netted zero from two opposite errors.
4. The general shape is addressed, not just five actions: state what happens at
   six and seven, and why the fix bounds the whole family rather than the next
   cell. An off-by-one that recurs at every odd action count is not fixed.
5. Consistency with the import-sourced path is stated — `#0336`'s exclusion
   handles those; say whether this fix subsumes it or sits alongside it.
6. Real `time.Now()` for every `Create`/`Commit`; order-independence proved with
   `-shuffle`, never `-count=N` (§5a). `#0324`'s first draft caused a cross-test
   flake exactly this way (`#0285`).
7. No migration needed; `000001`–`000022` frozen (§1), tree at `000025`.
