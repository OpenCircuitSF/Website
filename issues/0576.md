## Resolution

`OutreachPostCards.svelte` no longer prints `short_url` under the body; manual channels show one labelled "Link to paste" row with Copy link. The Instagram template ends "Sign up at the link in our bio." with no URL (#0570). The Instagram card offers "Open flyer". Decision: there is no flyer PNG anywhere in the system (flyers are printable HTML pages per location), so "Open flyer" links to the Flyers admin page (`adminOutreachFlyersHash()`), not to an image. TikTok and YouTube cards sit collapsed under a "Video channels" `<details>` per wave, and their template is a description block (title with date and venue, first summary sentence, sign-up link, site link).
