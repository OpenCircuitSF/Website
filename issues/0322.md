## Description

`#0304` set `claimKindsGuardMinPlausibleNonExemptCallSiteCount = 6` against 12
non-exempt `ClaimDue`/`OrphanSweep` call sites. `#0304`'s review measured which
narrowings that floor actually catches:

| Narrowing | Sites left | Caught by floor 6? |
|---|---|---|
| `{"."}` | 0 | yes |
| `cmd` only | 0 | yes |
| `internal/handlers` only | 5 | yes |
| worst single file | 3 | yes |
| **`internal/mailing` only** | **7** | **no** |

**The one that evades it is the one that matters most.** A `mailing`-only
narrowing drops `internal/handlers/subscribe_intake.go` — the production caller
the guard most exists for, since `#0254`'s duplicate-send defect came from an
unscoped sweep on exactly that path.

A floor of **8** closes it (`8 > 7`, `8 <= 12`).

## The trade-off to weigh

`#0275`'s failure mode is the opposite one: a floor set **above** its population
makes the guard impossible to pass, and that issue had to lower a floor from 150
to 80 for precisely that reason.

At 8 against 12 there are 4 sites of shrink headroom. Note that **3 of the 12 are
`mailing.SendStore`'s unrelated same-named `OrphanSweep`** — a rename or a
refactor removing those would leave 9, still above 8 but with only 1 to spare.

> **Correction (both reviews, measured twice — do not copy the paragraph above).**
> It is **four** of the twelve, not three: `internal/mailing/worker.go:575`
> (`Worker.store` is declared `*SendStore` at `internal/mailing/worker.go:262`)

> **zero** headroom, not one. This wrong figure came from `#0304`'s review, was
> repeated here, and `issues/0304.md`'s own recommendation still carries it.

So this is a judgement, not an arithmetic result. Make it deliberately.

### The defect: it is 4 of 12, not 3, and that costs the last site of headroom

The doc comment states:

> At 8 against 12 there are 4 sites of headroom — but **3 of the 12** are
> `mailing.SendStore`'s unrelated same-named `OrphanSweep` … would drop the
> population to **9**, leaving only 1 site of headroom.

Measured: **four** of the twelve target `SendStore.OrphanSweep`, not three.

| Site | Receiver | Which `OrphanSweep` |
|---|---|---|
| `internal/mailing/worker.go:575` | `Worker.store`, declared `*SendStore` | **SendStore's** |
| `internal/mailing/worker_store_test.go:109` | `store := NewSendStore(…)` | **SendStore's** |
| `internal/mailing/worker_store_test.go:151` | same | **SendStore's** |
| `internal/mailing/worker_store_test.go:165` | same | **SendStore's** |

`worker.go:575` is `w.store.OrphanSweep(ctx, c.ID, orphanStaleAfter)` —
`c.ID` is a campaign id, matching `SendStore.OrphanSweep(ctx, campaignID
int64, staleAfter time.Duration)` at `internal/mailing/worker_store.go:297`.
It is not an `outbox.Store` call. (`internal/mailing/outbox_worker.go`'s two
calls *are* `outbox.Store`'s — `OutboxWorker.store` is `*outbox.Store`.)

So the answer criterion 3 asks for is:

- a rename of `SendStore.OrphanSweep` drops the non-exempt population to **8**,
  not 9;
- that leaves **zero** sites of headroom, not one — the floor would sit exactly
  *at* the population, and any further shrink makes the guard unsatisfiable;
- equivalently: **8 is exactly the number of genuine, non-collision outbox call
  sites outside the package.** The entire margin this floor currently enjoys is
  supplied by an unrelated method that merely shares a name.

That is a materially different answer to the `#0275` question than the one
recorded, and it is the difference between "one step from the `#0275` failure
mode" and "at it".
