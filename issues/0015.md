## Description

The landing page every social link points at: a terminal hero block, a short statement of what Open Circuit SF is, the next few workshops, and an inline subscribe form.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Hero `TerminalPanel` composing `Prompt` (`> open_circuit // san_francisco`), a heavy uppercase display headline, a `StatusList`, and a `CommandLine`
- [ ] A short section stating what the group is and that workshops run at varied locations including neighborhood garages
- [ ] "Next up" section rendering up to three upcoming workshops (a static placeholder until #0053 provides the API)
- [ ] Inline `SubscribeForm` placeholder region wired in #0029
- [ ] Single `<h1>`; semantic `<header>`/`<main>`/`<footer>` landmarks
- [ ] Renders correctly at 375 px through 1440 px with no horizontal page scroll
- [ ] LCP under 2.0 s on a simulated 4G profile
- [ ] No mention of Sequoia Fabrica or any other venue as an affiliation

## Notes

PRD §4.1, §5.1, §7.6. The reference layout in PRD §4.1 establishes the composition — follow it rather than inventing a new arrangement.

Open Circuit SF is deliberately venue-independent. Copy should never imply a home space; "any location, including a neighborhood garage" is the positioning.

The headline is the LCP element. Keep the hero free of images and let type carry it.
