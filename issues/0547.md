# 0547 — Outreach does not track reactions or mentions on Bluesky and Mastodon

| | |
|---|---|

## Description

Phase 3b/4 of the Outreach design: poll engagement counts for posted
Bluesky/Mastodon posts into a time series, collect mentions/replies/quotes,
and show them in the panel and results view.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Migrations for `outreach_engagement` (`post_id`, `captured_at`, `likes`, `reposts`, `replies`, `quotes`) and `outreach_mentions` (`channel`, unique `remote_id`, nullable `post_id`, `kind`, `author`, `text`, `url`, `created_at`).
- [ ] Background poller: Bluesky `app.bsky.feed.getPosts` (public AppView), Mastodon `GET /api/v1/statuses/{id}`; hourly for 7 days after posting, daily to 30 days, then stop. Mentions: Bluesky `app.bsky.notification.listNotifications`, Mastodon `GET /api/v1/notifications`, every 15 minutes with a stored cursor; tie to a post when replying to/quoting it.
- [ ] Poller is disabled when the channel is not configured, logs (never silently `continue`s) on failure, and stops cleanly on shutdown (CLAUDE.md §5's `#0470` lesson).
- [ ] UI: reactions in panel cells and posts table; a Mentions and replies feed; reaction totals in the all-workshops table. The screen states Mastodon counts are as seen by our instance.
- [ ] Tests against fakes; verified with `ISSUE=0547 scripts/check.sh`.
