## Description

Add soldering topic guides to `/guides`, built on the topic-guide framework (`#0656`): catalog metadata in `web/src/content/guides/guides.json` (embedded and validated by `internal/guides`), page bodies in `web/src/lib/topics/topics.ts`, one interactive demo each.

Required topics (each with slug, title, hook, minutes, tags including `soldering`, and plain `post` copy for Bluesky/Mastodon; no soft hyphens or control characters):

- `soldering-solder-wire`: leaded 63/37 (eutectic, melts at 183 °C, easiest to learn) vs lead-free SAC305 (about 217–220 °C, more heat, duller finish); the rosin/no-clean flux core matters; 0.6–0.8 mm for electronics; never plumbing solder (acid flux corrodes).
- `soldering-iron-temperature`: about 330–350 °C leaded, 350–380 °C lead-free; too cold makes a dull, grainy cold joint and takes forever; too hot burns the flux off, oxidizes the tip and lifts pads; tip size and contact matter more than the dial; 2–3 s per joint.
- `soldering-tinning`: pre-tin the wire and the pad, then join them. Two pre-wetted surfaces flow together almost at once; untinned stranded wire wicks and frays and makes a weak blob.

Plus one to three more at the implementer's judgment (spot the bad joint, tip care, header pins on the ESP32-C3 SuperMini), with safety (flux fumes, washing hands after leaded solder, the stand, the iron staying hot) folded in.

Illustrations: no photos exist, so draw SVGs in `assets/guides/soldering/` and render 1x/2x PNGs into `web/public/img/guides/soldering/`, checked on light and dark backgrounds, with paths kept swappable for photos later. Logic in a plain TS module with unit tests; components thin; one jsdom behaviour test per new guide.

### Assumptions (user may override)

- **Six guides, not four.** All three optional ones were added; the header-pin guide ties straight to the workshop board.
- **Temperature model is illustrative**, not physics: chisel tip loses 15 °C into the joint, a fine tip 45 °C; the "good" window is the alloy's recommended dial range with a chisel tip. Dial ranges and melting points are the standard figures from the spec.
- **Tags** are `soldering` plus one more (`tools`, `heat`, `wires`, `debugging`, `ESP32`), matching the two-tag style of the existing topics.
- **No new OG cards or photos**; images are PNG (not WebP like the workshop photos) as the spec asked.
- **Art lives at `/img/guides/soldering/`**; a photo can replace a drawing by changing the one `art()` call in `soldering.ts`.
