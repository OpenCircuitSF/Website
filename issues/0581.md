1. Post now, Retry and Mark posted open an in-app `role="dialog"` (modelled
   on `OutreachScheduleAllDialog.svelte`) showing the post text, the
   account that will post it, and "public, cannot be unsent"; no
   `window.confirm` remains in `web/src/views/admin/Outreach*.svelte`
   (a source-level test pins it).
2. A **Post to Bluesky and Mastodon** button on the Announce wave posts both
   automatic Announce cards from one dialog and reports both results on one
   line.
3. Each card renders its own status / notice line under its button row (a
   live region per card); the page-level notice paragraph is removed; a
   notice is replaced when the card's state changes.
4. While a post is queued the card shows a visible progress indicator and
   the panel polls at 1 s, 2 s, 4 s then 10 s until the post resolves.
5. Copy text / Copy link flip their label to "Copied" for two seconds.
6. jsdom tests: dialog opens with the account and text; both-networks path
   calls `sendOutreachPost` twice; the per-card notice appears on the right
   card.

## Resolution

- **Go.** `outreachconn.Manager.Account(channel)` (Bluesky `@handle`, Mastodon
  instance host, else empty; no network); `AdminOutreachHandler.SetAccounts`
  and an `account` field on each channel view (automatic channels only);
  wired in `cmd/opencircuit/main.go` next to `SetHashtags`.
- **Lib.** `outreachCards.ts`: `pollDelay`, `postableAutomatic`,
  `CardNotice`/`visibleNotice`, `postDialogCopy`, `combinedPostResult`,
  `copyLabel`. `OutreachChannel.account` in `types.ts`.
- **Components.** New `OutreachConfirmDialog`, `OutreachPostDialog`,
  `OutreachPostCard`. `OutreachPostCards` is now the list: grouping, editing
  card, discard dialog, the Announce "Post to <A> and <B>" button (one dialog,
  sequential sends, one combined result line plus a notice on each card), and
  per-card notices. The page-level notice paragraph is gone; each card has its
  own `role="status"` and `role="alert"`. Queued cards show a spinner and
  "usually under ten seconds". Copy text / Copy link read "Copied" for 2 s.
  `OutreachComposer` uses the dialog for its discard prompt and, in card mode,
  no longer shows Post now / Retry / Mark posted (the card owns them behind its
  dialog). `OutreachPanel` polls at 1, 2, 4 then 10 s.
- **No native confirm** remains in `views/admin/Outreach*.svelte`; pinned by
  `outreachNoNativeConfirm.guard.test.ts`.
- **Deviations from the plan.** (1) The composer keeps Schedule / the picker in
  card mode: the card's "Schedule…" button opens the composer for its picker,
  so hiding it would remove scheduling; Pass 2 can revisit. (2) Per-card
  notices live in the list (a map keyed by post id) rather than inside each
  card, so the both-networks result can write to two cards; the card renders
  and writes them through `notice`/`onNotice`. (3) Copy also writes a
  "Copied." notice that clears after the same 2 s.
