What this is

Open Circuit SF is a San Francisco group running hands-on electronics workshops — soldering, microcontrollers, homelab, home automation, and whatever else the room wants to build next.

We are deliberately venue-independent. A workshop might run in a makerspace, a co-working room, or somebody’s garage. Bring curiosity; we bring the tools.

The full site is being built now — workshop listings and an interest-based mailing list are on the way. In the meantime, the Discord is where everything happens.
