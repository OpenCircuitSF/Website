Screen corners — dark photograph

Turn on the handles and drag them onto the four corners of the glass. The live canvas is projectively warped into that quadrilateral — real perspective, not a rotate/skew — and the corners are percentages of the photograph, so the fit survives a resize. The two photographs are not registered to each other, so each theme carries its own calibration; switch the theme to edit the other one.

Session — commands and output

Each step types its command, then prints its output lines. One output line per row in the box. Roughly 36 characters fit across the raster at the current font size; longer lines are not wrapped and will run off the glass, so anything over the limit is flagged.
