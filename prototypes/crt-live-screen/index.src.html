Calibration — prototype scaffolding

Turn on the handles and drag them onto the four corners of the photographed raster. The live canvas is projectively warped into that quadrilateral — real perspective, not a rotate/skew. Corners are percentages of the photograph, so the fit survives a resize. Paste the JSON below into the component when the numbers are right. The two photographs are registered to within 2px, so one calibration serves both themes — switch the theme and confirm rather than assuming.
